International

A US filing gives six months of priority (Paris Convention) to file abroad, for example through the Madrid Protocol. Decide by about April 2027 for marks filed now. EU, UK, Canada and Japan are typical first picks for software.

3. How the sites mark names today

  • All four site footers: "MOQOM™ … are trademarks of Elliott Wave LLC." The ™ symbol is correct for unregistered, claimed marks.
  • Legal pages say none of the marks is registered yet and ® will be used only after registration. Using ® before registration is illegal (15 U.S.C. §1111 and §1125(a) false-designation risk).
  • Don't say "trademark pending" or "registration pending" until an application is actually filed. After filing it is accurate, but ™ remains the right symbol.
